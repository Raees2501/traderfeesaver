What Happened to FTX?

FTX collapsed in November 2022 after it emerged that customer deposits had been secretly funneled to its sister trading firm, Alameda Research. A bank-run followed, the exchange froze withdrawals, and it filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy within days. Founder Sam Bankman-Fried was convicted of fraud in 2023 and sentenced to 25 years in prison in 2024.

  • November 2022: Withdrawals frozen; FTX files for bankruptcy.
