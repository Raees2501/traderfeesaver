How prop firm payouts actually reach Pakistan

This is where Pakistani traders get stuck, and where most "best prop firm" lists say nothing useful. Here are the realistic routes, best first:

1. Crypto (USDT or USDC)

The most common route. The firm sends stablecoins to your wallet, you move them to an exchange, and you sell for rupees through P2P into your own bank account or wallet. It's fast and cheap, and you control every step. The one thing to watch is the network: some firms pay only on a specific chain, such as ERC20, so check your receiving address supports it before requesting a payout.

2. Rise

Several firms, FXIFY among them according to third-party summaries, pay through Rise, a payroll platform that can forward money to a bank account or to crypto. Rise may charge its own withdrawal fee, separate from the firm.

3. International bank wire

It works, but it's slow (often several business days) and gets eaten by intermediary and receiving-bank charges. Your bank may also ask about the source of funds. It's best kept for large, infrequent withdrawals.

💡 Turning USDT into rupees

Sell on Binance P2P to a verified buyer, and only release the USDT after the rupees have actually arrived in an account in your name. The safety rules in our USDT Pakistan guide apply in reverse when you're selling, and the risk of frozen accounts is higher on the selling side, so be strict about who you trade with.

Checklist before you pay for a challenge from Pakistan

1

Confirm KYC accepts your documents

You'll need to pass identity checks before your first payout, not just at signup. Make sure a CNIC or Pakistani passport is accepted.

2

Confirm the payout route works for you

Find the firm's payout-methods page and make sure at least one option, usually crypto or Rise, reaches you. If the only option is something unavailable in Pakistan, walk away.

3

Check how you'll pay for the challenge

Many Pakistani debit cards block or cap international online payments by default. Enable international e-commerce in your banking app first, or check whether the firm accepts crypto payment.

4

Read the rules that fail people

Check the daily drawdown calculation (balance or equity based), news-trading restrictions, weekend holding, and any consistency rule. These end more challenges than the profit target does.

5

Budget the fee as a loss

The large majority of challenge attempts fail. Size the challenge so that losing the fee doesn't hurt, and don't buy a bigger account to "make it back".

Tax on prop firm payouts in Pakistan

Prop payouts are foreign-sourced income, and Pakistan's tax treatment of them isn't settled in plain-English guidance. Keep every payout record and every P2P conversion receipt, and if your payouts become regular, speak to a Pakistani tax practitioner before filing season rather than after it.
