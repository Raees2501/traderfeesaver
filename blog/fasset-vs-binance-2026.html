Halal structure: the real reason people compare these two

Fasset was built around Shariah principles. It doesn't offer leveraged derivatives or interest-bearing products, and it markets itself as a stablecoin-powered Islamic platform. Binance offers spot trading alongside futures, margin and yield products, and most scholars treat the leveraged and interest-based ones as impermissible. You can use Binance for plain spot trading only, but you have to police that yourself.

⚠ No universal certification exists

There is no single global Shariah certification for crypto platforms, and AAOIFI has not issued a final crypto standard. Fasset's product design removes the most commonly objected-to features. That's a meaningful difference, but it isn't a fatwa. Our Is Fasset halal? page goes through the details.

Getting rupees in and out

Both platforms move PKR through peer-to-peer counterparties rather than direct bank integration. The difference is scale.

  • Binance P2P has the most sellers and the tightest spreads. A late-October 2026 survey of its PKR adverts found Easypaisa, bank transfers, Raast, SadaPay, NayaPay and Upaisa accepted. Step-by-step instructions are in our USDT buying guide.
  • Fasset routes rupees through an in-app merchant network: you pay a merchant's bank account in PKR and receive USDT. Fasset doesn't add its own fee, but the merchant rate can be a little wider than Binance's best P2P offers.

If Fasset's merchant rate is noticeably worse on the day, you can buy USDT on Binance P2P and send it to your Fasset deposit address. First check which networks Fasset lists on its deposit screen, and send on one that both sides support.

Fees

Binance's standard spot fee is 0.1% per trade, with a discount for paying fees in BNB, and our referral code 775069017 adds a further trading-fee discount. P2P is free for buyers. Fasset shows the full cost of each buy or sell on the confirmation screen, so compare that number against Binance's 0.1% for the same trade size before you decide.

For the card, Fasset charges a one-time 9.99 USDT activation, which promo codes have reduced or waived in the past (see current codes). Top-ups and per-transaction fees have been free during Fasset's promo phase.

What you can actually buy

Binance wins on crypto variety by a wide margin. If you want small-cap tokens, new listings or derivatives, Fasset isn't built for that. Fasset's edge is outside crypto: from a Pakistani account you can reach US stocks, REITs and S&P 500 ETFs, plus tokenised gold. That makes it closer to a USD savings and investing app than an exchange, and it's why Fasset is more often compared with Payoneer than with Binance.
