What Happened to Bittrex?

Bittrex was founded in 2014 in Seattle and built its reputation on security and a huge altcoin catalog. Its downfall was regulatory, not a hack or fraud:

  • April 2023: The SEC charged Bittrex with operating an unregistered securities exchange, broker, and clearing agency.
  • May 2023: Bittrex US filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy and wound down its American operations.
  • December 2023: Bittrex Global — the international arm that served the rest of the world — announced it was winding down too, and trading ceased on December 4, 2023.

Unlike FTX, Bittrex's shutdown was orderly: customers were given withdrawal windows. But those windows closed long ago, and the exchange is permanently gone.
