Fasset Card: best for halal-minded users and freelancers

The Fasset Card is a virtual Visa prepaid card that you top up in USDT from your Fasset wallet and spend in USD. Pakistan is not on Fasset's published card-issuance restriction list, which names Russia, China, India, the UAE, Brazil and Nigeria. You still need to confirm eligibility in the app before paying.

  • Activation: 9.99 USDT, one-time and non-refundable. Fasset has promoted codes that cut or waive this fee, and our Fasset promo code page explains how to check whether one is live.
  • Limits: top-ups from 1 to 5,000 USDT per transaction, with a $2,000 daily spending cap.
  • Fees: free top-ups and no per-transaction fee during Fasset's promotional phase.
  • Why it stands out: the same app gives you a PKR on-ramp through its merchant network, Shariah-structured products, and access to US stocks and ETFs.

Because it's virtual-only, there's no ATM cash withdrawal. To turn USDT back into rupees, you go through the merchant P2P flow. The full activation walkthrough is in our Fasset card activation guide.

KAST: best free card with cashback

KAST's Standard tier issues a free virtual Visa, with physical cards on paid tiers. Cashback dropped to 1.5% in May 2026, capped at $2,000 of monthly spend. A premium tier pays 2% for an annual fee. FX markup runs 0.5–1.75% depending on the transaction, with no extra conversion fee.

KAST is the most generous option if you spend steadily in USD and don't want an upfront fee. Sign up with code 6K8MW88A, and confirm Pakistan eligibility during onboarding. Third-party roundups list it for Pakistan, but we haven't seen KAST publish an explicit country statement.

OKX Card: best if you already trade on OKX

OKX Card is a virtual Mastercard funded from your OKX Pay balance. It's free to issue, and ProPakistani reports that Pakistani users can complete KYC with a CNIC. A physical card isn't offered in Pakistan. It charges no FX markup on USDG-denominated spending, and cashback scales from 4% to 10% with your OKX VIP tier, so most new users will sit at the bottom of that range.

It suits people who already buy USDT through OKX P2P. Create your account with OKX code 37031620.

RedotPay: widest coin support, highest spending cost

RedotPay accepts top-ups in USDT, USDC, BTC and ETH and is available to Pakistani users after KYC. The catch is cost. The virtual card is $10 and the physical card $100, and every non-USD purchase carries a 1.2% FX markup plus a 1% conversion fee. At $500 a month of spending, that's roughly $130 a year in fees before the card fee. There's no cashback on the free plan.

Using a crypto card in Pakistan: what to expect

  • It's a dollar card. These cards settle in USD. Spending at a Pakistani shop in rupees triggers the issuer's FX markup, so the cards make the most sense for USD-priced things: subscriptions, software, hosting, ads, and international shopping.
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported by several issuers, which makes a virtual card usable for tap-to-pay.
  • Legal position: crypto cards aren't directly licensed under Pakistan's new virtual-asset framework yet, and personal use isn't explicitly banned. The Fasset Pakistan guide covers what the Virtual Assets Act 2026 changed.
  • Funding the card means getting USDT first. Our guide to buying USDT in Pakistan covers the cheapest P2P routes.
